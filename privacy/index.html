Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

Pirates of Ybor is run by Adam Kornreich / R.A. Consulting. This policy covers thepiratesofybor.com, the Pirates of Ybor app on the Apple App Store, Google Play, Amazon Appstore and Samsung Galaxy Store, and every game inside them, including Gaspar's Grotto Arcade and Andy Seavy Golf Club.

Playing without an account

You never need an account to play. Without one, games save your scores, streaks, doubloons, settings and your 21+ answer in your own device's storage. That stays on your device and is not sent to us.

On this website

This website has no sign-in. Scores and settings stay in your own browser on your own device.

If you create an account in the app

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook. We do not see or store your password. We collect:

  • Account details: a user ID, the email address the sign-in provider shares (Apple lets you hide it), and your display name and avatar. If you choose a provider photo as your avatar, we store its web address.
  • Game scores: the game, your score and when you played. This keeps your best scores on all your devices.
  • Purchases: which store item you own, which store it came from, the store's transaction ID and when a subscription ends. Apple, Google or Amazon handle the payment itself; we never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Trivia answers: when you answer a question from our fresh question bank, we count whether it was answered right or wrong so we can fix questions that are too hard or wrong. This count is not linked to you.

We use this information only to run the games and your account. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Who processes the data for us

  • Supabase stores accounts, scores, purchase records and the trivia question bank.
  • RevenueCat confirms store purchases and subscriptions with Apple, Google and Amazon, using your account's user ID.
  • Apple, Google and Facebook handle sign-in when you choose them; Apple, Google and Amazon handle payments.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces, and may see your IP address when a font loads.

Sound, camera and recordings

Some games play music and narration using your device's own text-to-speech. If a game offers to record a clip of your play, it is made on your device and saved only if you choose to save it. The app does not use your microphone, contacts or location.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep account data while your account exists. You can delete your account any time from Account → Delete my account in the app or on the website; that permanently removes your profile, scores and purchase records from our servers. See Delete My Data for other ways.

Children and age

Pirates of Ybor is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13; we do not knowingly collect information from them. Drink-themed games (the Pour-Off and the tavern drinking games) are for ages 21 and up and ask your age before you play.

People shown in the games

Real people appear only with their permission. Mentioning a person or brand is not an endorsement; see our no-endorsement notice.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date, and tell signed-in players in the app about major changes.
