About the krewe

Pirates of Ybor is a social krewe. We gather for parades, festivals and community events across Tampa Bay, and for plenty of good times in between.

Sail together

Parades, invasions and street festivals, in full pirate garb.

Give back

We put our plunder to work for local causes in and around Ybor.

Make friends

Socials, meetups and a crew that shows up for each other.
