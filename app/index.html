The App

Pirates of Ybor, presented by Gaspar's Grotto

The whole wreck in your pocket: the Game Room, the Movie Room, the Family Room and Gaspar's Grotto tavern, on your phone or tablet, with your scores on every device.

Get it

Buttons switch on the day each store approves the app.

What the app adds

  • Sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook (optional; every game plays without it)
  • Your best scores saved across devices
  • Fresh trivia questions every night
  • Store extras: the Ybor Links Back Nine for Andy Seavy Golf Club, tournament entries and the Crew Pass

Play now, no download

Everything already plays free in your browser. On iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen; on Android, tap the menu and Install app or Add to Home screen.
