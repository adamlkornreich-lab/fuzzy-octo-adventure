Open World

Walk Riviera Ave and find Laura & Dom's Crossword Cabin on Cross St, cross the bridge to Jen's Island, visit Ruben's Cliff and Rene's Guitar Shack, and head down to the Dragon Table at Acropolis on the 7th Ave block. Enter the open world.

Online matchmaking

Player-vs-player across the internet arrives with crew accounts and the app-store release. Until then, the tavern games, chess, trivia and Shere's Revenge support two players on one device. Andy Golf Club, Treasure Hunt, the crossword, Jen Runs the Ship, The Schiller Ledger , the Rumor Mill and Rene's Guitar School are solo.
