Delete your account

Signed in? Open Account in the app and tap Delete my account, then type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your profile, your scores, your purchase records and your sign-in from our servers, right away. It works the same in the app and on the website.

A Crew Pass subscription is billed by Apple, Google or Amazon, so cancel it in your store's subscription settings as well. Deleting your account does not refund past purchases; ask your store about refunds.

Erase what's saved on this device

Scores and settings saved on this device (signed in or not) can be erased with the button below, or by clearing site data for thepiratesofybor.com in your browser, or by deleting the app.

Can't sign in any more?

Email adamlkornreich@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" from the email address on your account. We will delete everything and confirm within 30 days.
