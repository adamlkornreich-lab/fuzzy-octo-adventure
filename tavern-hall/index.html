Pirate Trivia

Five questions a round. Tampa, Ybor and Florida, pirates and the sea, the movies, and general knowledge.

Find the Treasure Chest

The chest is buried under one square. Dig, and the colour tells you how far you are. Eight shovels, one chest.

Red: right next to it · Amber: close · Teal: far · Blue: freezing

Pirate Chess

You sail white. The AI sails black. Tap a piece, then tap where it goes.

Dedicated to Evan Schiller's baby, a future chess master.

Pirate Darts

The crosshair swings across the board. Tap the board to throw. Three darts a turn, five rounds. Triple and double rings count, bull is 50.

Ping Pong

Drag on the table to move your paddle. In two player mode the left half of the table moves the left paddle and the right half moves the right one. First to 7.

0 : 0

Andy Seavy Golf Club

Drag back from the ball like a slingshot, then let go. Longer drag, harder hit. Sand slows the ball. Water costs a stroke and puts you back where you last hit from.

Shere's Revenge

Duel three pirates. Strike beats Feint, Parry beats Strike, Feint beats Parry. Read your rival and win the round.

SHERE
ENEMY

Cup Pong

Tap the table when the crosshair sits over a cup. Sink all six of your rival's cups first. You shoot at the top cups, your rival at the bottom ones.

Flip Cup

First to fifteen flips wins. Mash your button as fast as you can. In two player mode each player takes a button. Space bar works for player one.

PLAYER 1
CAPTAIN AI

Shuffleboard

Drag back from your puck and let go to slide it. Four pucks each, taking turns. Pucks in the far zone score 3, the next 2, the next 1. Go past the end and it's gone. Pucks knock each other about.

Bowling

Tap once to lock your aim as the marker slides across the lane, then tap again to lock your power. Aim for the middle and about four fifths power. Five frames per player.

Pirate Bingo

Tap a number on your card once the caller has drawn it. Spot a full row, column or diagonal and shout BINGO before the two AI cards do. The middle square is free.
