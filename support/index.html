Quick fixes

  • No sound: tap the speaker icon in the game, and check your phone is not on silent.
  • Game looks stuck: reload the page. Scores saved on your device will still be there.
  • Scores disappeared: private browsing and clearing site data both erase saved scores.
  • Playing on a TV: games marked TV remote ready work with arrow keys and Enter.

Report a question or a problem

Spotted a wrong trivia answer or something that should not be there? Email us the game and the question and we will fix it.
