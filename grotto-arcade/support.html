How to play

GameTouchKeyboard or TV remote
Andy Seavy Golf ClubTap Swing three times: start, set power, stop on the white line. ◂ ▸ aims, ‹ › changes club.Arrows aim and change club, Enter swings
The Pour-OffHold a bottle to pour, let go at the gold line. Dump starts the glass over.Hold 1–8 to pour, Backspace dumps
Whack-a-KrakenTap krakens and doubloons, not Pippy.Number pad 1–9
Tavern Games, ClassicsTap the buttons on each game.Tab and Enter

Every game has a ◂ tab on the left edge to return to the arcade. On a TV remote, Back does the same.

Sound

In Andy Seavy Golf Club and the Pour-Off, 🔊 turns effects on or off, ♫ switches between West Coast beats, East Coast beats and silence, and 🎙 turns the announcer on or off. Sound starts after your first tap.

Reset your scores

Scores live only on your device. To start fresh, clear the app's data in your device settings, or delete and reinstall the app.
